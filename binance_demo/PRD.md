# PRD — K Signal Binance Demo Trading v1.3

## Goal
Research-grade USD-M Binance Demo integration, separate from `k-signal-paper-monitor`. Use Demo virtual assets only. Pinned host `demo-fapi.binance.com`; production Binance calls are prohibited.

## Features
1. Monitor public `KCryptoBTC168` Telegram messages every minute; first scan is a baseline, never backfill old orders.
2. Accept fresh explicit BTC/ETH/SOL/ZEC direction+entry+stop signals. Quote/lot filters and One-Way position mode required.
3. Demo signed MARKET/LIMIT order, deterministic client IDs and durable write-ahead intent before every entry, cancel, stop and TP mutation. A timeout/5xx outcome is unknown and must be queried by the same client ID; never blindly resubmit.
4. Reconcile entry orders with `GET /fapi/v1/order`, account-wide and symbol `GET /fapi/v3/positionRisk`, and conditional stops with `GET /fapi/v1/algoOrder`. Reject Hedge Mode and halt on unmanaged positions.
5. After any partial fill, first confirm an exchange-side `STOP_MARKET closePosition=true` Algo Order, then cancel the unfilled entry remainder. Cancel all still-open LIMIT entries at 24 hours and before TP/flat settlement.
6. Conservative Demo TP: include actual entry fees from `/userTrades`, Funding Income from `/income`, estimated exit fee/slippage and best bid/ask. Submit exactly one reduceOnly exit only when projected net PnL >= 1% of filled entry notional.
7. Final settlement requires owned entry and exit fills. Report actual realized PnL minus USDT commissions plus USDT funding; never label a non-USDT fee as USDT profit. Cancel a still-active protective stop after a TP close before allowing another entry.
8. Risk: Isolated 1x, cap 50 USDT default / 100 max, 0.5% demo account risk, one managed open trade. Halt on auth, persistence, protection, position ownership, reconciliation or uncertain cleanup failures; apply 429/5xx backoff.
9. Dedicated Bun Railway Function and 512MB volume; private Basic Auth dashboard and durable outbound Telegram outbox. Every trade notification is visibly prefixed `BINANCE DEMO` and includes exchange/client IDs and confirmed status when available.
10. Execution gates are source-scoped: `DEMO_ORDER_ENABLED=true` permits new Telegram-channel candidates, while `DEMO_DONCHIAN_ORDER_ENABLED=true` permits only Donchian entries/exits. Enabling Donchian must never arm channel candidates. Disabling either entry gate never disables protective reconciliation of an existing managed Demo position. Never require or accept production keys.
11. Independent Donchian engine for BTC/ETH/SOL/ZEC uses Binance Demo public 15-minute **closed** klines. A close above/below the prior 20-bar channel signals long/short entry; a later close at the fixed stop/2R target or through the moving 10-bar opposite channel signals exit. Notifications show pair, direction, entry, stop, target, risk, exit reason and a pair-specific TradingView perpetual-contract chart URL preselected to 15m, avoiding reliance on the region-blocked Binance website. It maintains separate state, baselines the first observation, deduplicates by close time and exposes `/api/donchian`.
12. When the Donchian execution gate, Demo-only credentials, storage and account safety checks are all ready, an entry signal creates a source-tagged MARKET candidate with a 0.5% maximum reference-price deviation, the signal-time stop and 2R strategy target. It reuses One-Way/isolated-1x sizing, deterministic IDs, write-ahead persistence, exchange stop, partial-fill and reconciliation. A Donchian exit cancels an active entry remainder and submits one deterministic reduceOnly MARKET close; the existing fee-adjusted net +1% exit may close first. Completed exchange fills persist weighted entry/exit prices, commission, funding, gross and actual net PnL and are exposed by protected `/api/trades`.
13. Paper Monitor consumes a token-protected, read-only `/api/stats` Donchian aggregate. Its dashboard renders two visibly separate accounts: `Telegram 訊號 Paper 帳本` and `Donchian 策略 Paper 帳本`, each with its own 10,000 USDT starting balance and net asset value. It must not show a combined net asset value or cross-ledger total. Telegram Paper return/equity curve remain unchanged. The aggregate excludes credentials and order identities; a missing/invalid upstream must not break Paper reporting.
14. Donchian Paper positions remain durable after closing. The sanitized aggregate exposes the latest 100 closed Paper records without internal IDs, credentials or exchange-order identities. Paper Monitor displays symbol, side, reference and simulated fill prices, exit reason, fees, fee-adjusted net PnL, close time and chart link in a separate closed-history table.
15. Donchian entry and exit decisions use only closed candles for the position's strategy interval (Legacy 15m or active 1h). Separately, open-position reporting and unrealized Paper PnL use Binance Demo public mark prices refreshed by the Donchian poll. The dashboard labels this field `即時標記價`, includes the observation timestamp and never presents a closed-candle price as current market price.
16. New Donchian Paper entries use `DONCHIAN_1H_20_10_EMA200_ATR14_2X_2R`: closed 1-hour candles, 20-period breakout, long only above EMA200 and short only below EMA200, fixed signal-time 2×ATR14 stop, fixed 2R target and 10-period channel exit. Existing open `DONCHIAN_15M_20_10_2R` Paper positions remain grandfathered under their original 15-minute exit rules; no position is force-closed or silently reinterpreted during migration. All new strategy activity remains Paper-only.
17. Every Donchian entry opens a separate 100 USDT-notional Paper position using adverse 0.02% entry/exit slippage and 0.04% per-side fees. The report shows symbol, direction, reference entry, current price, stop, target, unrealized PnL, signal time and TradingView chart at the position's strategy interval. A Donchian exit closes and records fee-adjusted Paper PnL. This sub-ledger remains separate from Telegram Paper and Binance Demo fills.
18. Donchian notifications and dashboard headings are labelled `DONCHIAN PAPER`. They state that the Paper position was created or settled and that no Binance Demo or production order was submitted. `BINANCE DEMO` remains reserved for actual exchange-order lifecycle notices.
19. Every Telegram Paper trade row includes a `K線` link to the matching TradingView perpetual-contract symbol at 15m. The link is read-only reporting metadata and must not affect Paper state, fills, PnL or either Demo execution gate.
20. Telegram Paper trade history is paginated at 10 records per page within the existing latest-100 display window. The dashboard provides previous/next controls and current/total page numbers, clamps the page when refreshed data changes, and does not mutate the underlying ledger.
21. Paper Monitor's dashboard, sanitized report/status/order data, Telegram delivery summary and CSV export are publicly readable without Basic Auth. Telegram pairing setup, self-test, raw captured messages and raw quote diagnostics remain Basic-Auth protected. The public Telegram status excludes pairing codes, setup URLs and operational error details.
22. The dashboard is responsive: one-column summary cards on phones, all record tables render as labelled mobile cards instead of wide horizontal tables, and pagination controls wrap within the viewport.

## Non-goals / outstanding
No live money and no full high-frequency reliability claim. Donchian signals are mechanical indicators, not profitability promises. Telegram pagination, user-data WebSocket reconciliation, non-USDT fee conversion, exchange-side integration/fault tests and a 7–30 day soak still require acceptance. REST history windows are bounded; long-lived positions need pagination before production-like use.
